# UC.SV.01 — Screensaver pictures

Related requirements: FR.6.9. Test cases: [TC.SV.01](/docs/testcases/TC.SV.01-Screensaver.md).

## UC.SV.01 — Watch the screensaver slideshow
**Actor:** the User. **Precondition:** a picture folder is set in Settings › Screensaver and the idle time has passed.
**Main flow:** 1. The cover comes up and shows a picture of the folder. 2. The picture covers the whole screen; where its ratio differs from the screen's, the overflow is cropped at the centre; no bars. 3. The next picture follows after the slide interval.
**Covers:** FR.6.9.
